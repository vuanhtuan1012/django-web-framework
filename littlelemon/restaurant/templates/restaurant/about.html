Our Story

Based in Chicago, Illinois, Little Lemon is a family-owned Mediterranean restaurant, focused on traditional recipes served with a modern twist.

The chefs draw inspiration from Italian, Greek, and Turkish culture and curate a menu of 12-15 dishes that rotate seasonally to highlight fresh ingredients.

The restaurant offers a rustic and relaxed atmosphere with moderate prices, making it a popular destination for a meal any time of the day.

Little Lemon is owned by two Italian brothers, Mario and Adrian, who moved to the United States to pursue their shared dream of owning a restaurant.

Mario relies on family recipes and his experience as a chef in Italy to craft the menu, while Adrian leads the marketing efforts and expanded the menu beyond classic Italian cuisine to include flavors from across the Mediterranean region.
